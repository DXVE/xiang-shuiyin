Replaceability (LGPLv3 section 4(d)(1))
---------------------------------------
  The distributed Windows build is an installer that lays out a normal folder:
  the Qt libraries sit next to the application and can be replaced in place by
  the end user, which is the mechanism of section 4(d)(1). The source
  availability stated above remains available as the fallback of section
  4(d)(0). A build that packs the Qt libraries inside a single-file executable
  cannot be replaced in place and would therefore rely on the source
  availability alone.
